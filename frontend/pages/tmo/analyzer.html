TRANSMISSION & MARKET OPERATIONS INTELLIGENCE

One reconciled operational picture from three official sources.

Actual 33 kV system offtake and feeder loading are reconciled with 11 kV downstream service performance and the approved Forecast/Target workbook. 33 kV and 11 kV energy are kept as separate network views to prevent double-counting.

How the TMO analysis is reconciled: The Forecast workbook is a KEDCO system-level hourly target and is compared hour-by-hour with the validated 33 kV TOTAL ENERGY OFFTAKE. Feeder, station, Region, Band and customer-segment performance are calculated from the 33 kV / 11 kV operational workbooks. The forecast is not artificially split among feeders or Regions unless an approved source workbook supplies feeder/Region targets. Customer-segment grouping comes from the embedded CONTROLLED TMO FEEDER MASTER.xlsx: Regions, MD Industrial (MDI) and MD Non-Industrial (MDNI). Transformer MVA is read from the same controlled database where stated; contradictory source classifications are flagged instead of silently overwritten.
